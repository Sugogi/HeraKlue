HeraKlue v13 changes

- Poseidon now spawns as soon as the Ariadne tutorial begins.
- Poseidon is persistent from the tutorial onward and is not removed between story steps.
- Poseidon spawns several meters away and slightly to the user's right so he does not sit directly behind Ariadne during the tutorial.
- After Ariadne's tutorial, the Poseidon mission flow now requires the player to aim the crosshair at Poseidon to see/hear his dialogue and continue.
- Updated the final Ariadne tutorial line so it tells the player Poseidon is nearby for the first mission.
- Kept previous behavior: Ariadne dialogue requires aiming at Ariadne, Poseidon dialogue requires aiming at Poseidon, mission/objective prompts are not crosshair-locked, and marker-scanned Ariadne/puzzle behavior is preserved.
