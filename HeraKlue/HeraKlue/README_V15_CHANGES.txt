HeraKlue v15 changes

- Accept Mission is still locked to Poseidon: the accept screen only stays visible while the crosshair is pointed at Poseidon.
- Added proximity gating for Poseidon dialogue.
- Poseidon messages only appear when the crosshair is aimed at Poseidon and the player is within about 2.25 meters.
- If the player aims at Poseidon from too far away, the prompt says: Move closer to Poseidon.
- Mission/objective prompts remain independent of crosshair targeting except the special Accept Mission step.
- Poseidon still spawns during the Ariadne tutorial and remains persistent.
